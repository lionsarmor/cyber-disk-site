R05 fit review

The right-loading Cyber Disk tucks about 10 mm beneath the F87 tray. About 20 mm of the motherboard also sits under the tray. The cartridge loads from the left. In Mounts and packaging, turn on clearance zones to inspect the overlaps.

The board outline, holes and EXP1 registration follow the v1.1 iBOM. Connector heights and Wi-Fi fit still need a physical check. The flat Wi-Fi card sits over EXP2 and EXP3.
