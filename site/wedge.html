R09 fit review

The F87 has a nominal 0.6 mm gap per edge. The opening is cut into the deck. Lift the keyboard along the deck normal, about 14° forward of vertical.

The Cyber Disk stays external. Use Rear I/O to read the cutouts head-on and Diagonal edges to inspect the faceted rail. The Magnetic cover view lifts the cover clear of the case.

The onboard ports follow the v1.1 board layout. The rounded openings, separate expansion window and power placement use the short X16-P V2 as a reference. Check the F87 contour and folded feet, magnet retention, connector fit, and Wi-Fi stack on the hardware before cutting. The flat Wi-Fi card sits over EXP2 and EXP3.
