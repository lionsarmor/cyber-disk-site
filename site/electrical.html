Drive emulation needs more than a connector.

Prototype design — not ready to order as a working drive. Main controller and daughterboard are still unrouted. Contact power isolation, hardware interlock and firmware are unfinished. Static checks below do not prove powered operation or computer compatibility.

Corrections in this pass

  • Floppy reset outputs: changed RN3 from 10 kΩ to 1 kΩ. The previous network could not guarantee a LOW at the LS06 inputs.
  • Media enable default: used a spare RN3 leg for a stronger 1 kΩ ARM pulldown and removed separate R3. The core now has 20 component positions.
  • Dock receiver: used a spare U2 channel and RN3 leg for a buffered, defined disconnected input. No added IC or cable change. Pico 2 GPIO1 already has a 3.3 V unpowered failsafe rating; this is receiver hardening, not a confirmed backfeed repair.

What still blocks samples

  • Moving contacts: the 7-wire passive spring board cannot connect directly to core J3. Complete switched media power, signal isolation and hardware seating/eject sequencing.
  • Write durability: spring eject and power loss can interrupt flash operations. Define recoverable commits and test interrupted writes.
  • Power and disconnected states: close the 5 V voltage-drop budget, dock input bias, peer power-off behavior and ESD protection. A nominal 5 V supply alone does not guarantee 4.75 V at the TTL chips.
  • Manufacturing: qualify connector footprints and harnesses, route both carrier boards, then repeat the review and release new prototype fabrication files.
  • Firmware: prove real read/write/format, disk-change and error behavior. Only one host may own writable media at a time.
